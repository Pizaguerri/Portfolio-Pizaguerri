### This is my portfolio in HUGO.

This is my photography portfolio and blog to substitute instagram. I learned not long ago that I don't need a hosting service to make my own portfolio. 
When I discovered static webpages and HUGO, I thought I could somehow crete my own website with a template, add to it whatever I wanted and even have my own blog.
The idea is to eliminate the need of the hosting subscription, because I don't expect a lot of real traffic, and to have a blog in order to give life to my photography and thoughts in Obsidian.
So, this is it.
